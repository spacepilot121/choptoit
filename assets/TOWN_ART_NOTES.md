# Town environment artwork


## Story destinations, v2

Built-in image generation. Saved in `assets/durham-v2.png`, `assets/chester-v2.png`, and `assets/london-v2.png`; original city images retained. Shared portrait composition and palette, with distinct cathedral, sandstone gate and capital riverfront scenes. Painted stage surfaces differ slightly in height; per-city offsets in `cityBackdropOffsets` align them with the gameplay baseline.

### durham

Prompt: Use case: illustration-story. Create a production background for a portrait 9:16 mobile arcade game, Chop To It. Polished hand-painted indie game illustration, chunky geometric forms, restrained pale teal sky, deep teal shadows, warm ochre stone and wood, subtle paper grain. Charming medieval dark comedy. Full bleed 9:16 portrait. No text, UI, people, animals, weapons or moving gameplay targets. The upper 50 percent is mostly uncluttered pale teal sky for flying game objects. Buildings frame the sides below this, a low-contrast distant skyline in the middle. A simple horizontal wooden performance stage crosses the full width at exactly 77 percent of image height; cobblestone square beneath it. Side-on game perspective, readable silhouettes, atmospheric depth. Keep the central play area open. Destination: Durham: an imposing but distant Norman cathedral with square towers sits on a green hill behind a bend in the river. Honey stone buildings and mossy retaining walls frame the square. Quiet northern cathedral town; a few red cloth awnings near the edges. Cathedral silhouette stays below the upper open sky.
